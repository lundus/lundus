# AI-Based Fire Detector Placement Optimization

**Master's thesis project** | MSc in Computer Science (Robotics specialization), Óbuda University  
**Status:** Work in progress (2026)

## Project overview

I am developing an offline, AI-assisted engineering application to support fire detector placement using architectural CAD floor plans.

The project brings together my professional experience in fire detection system design and my studies in computer science, software engineering, and machine learning. Its aim is to investigate how CAD data processing, AI-assisted methods, and domain-specific engineering requirements can support a practical design workflow.

## Areas of focus

- Processing architectural CAD/DXF data for engineering use.
- Exploring machine learning for design-support tasks.
- Considering domain-specific technical requirements and engineering review.
- Building and evaluating an application intended to operate offline.

## Technologies

**Python** · **PyTorch** · **Machine Learning** · **CAD/DXF** · **Docker** · **Git** · **Jupyter**
